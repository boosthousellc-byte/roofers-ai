Every missed call is a missed job. And if you're running a roofing company, you know how many calls come in while you're on a roof, driving to a site, or eating dinner with your family. The average roofing company misses 40-60% of incoming calls during business hours. After hours? That number jumps to nearly 100%.

An AI chatbot changes that equation entirely. It responds to every website visitor in under 3 seconds, qualifies the lead, and books the estimate — all without you lifting a finger.

## What Is a Roofing Chatbot?

A roofing chatbot is an AI-powered conversational tool embedded on your website that engages visitors in real-time. Unlike basic chat widgets that just collect a name and email, an AI chatbot trained on roofing industry knowledge can have intelligent conversations about roof types, storm damage, insurance claims, and repair timelines.

It's like having your best salesperson available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — except this one never calls in sick, never has a bad day, and handles 50 conversations at once.

## How Does the AI Qualify Roofing Leads?

The chatbot follows a proven qualification flow designed specifically for roofing companies:

1. **Greets the visitor** — "Hi! Looking for a roof repair or replacement? I can help you get a free estimate."
2. **Identifies the project** — Asks about roof type (shingle, tile, metal), the nature of the issue (leak, storm damage, age), and the scope (repair vs. full replacement)
3. **Captures location** — Confirms the service area to ensure it's a viable lead
4. **Gauges timeline** — "When are you looking to get this done?" helps prioritize urgent leads
5. **Books the estimate** — Integrates with your calendar to schedule on the spot

Every answer is scored, and high-quality leads are flagged for immediate attention. Low-quality or out-of-area inquiries are handled politely without wasting your time.

## Do AI Chatbots Work for Small Roofing Companies?

Absolutely. AI chatbots are actually more impactful for small roofing companies than large ones. Here's why:

- **Small companies miss more calls** — With fewer office staff, more calls go unanswered
- **Every lead matters more** — When you're doing 10-20 jobs a month, one lost lead is significant
- **It replaces hiring** — An AI chatbot does the work of a full-time receptionist at a fraction of the cost
- **It levels the playing field** — Small roofers can offer the same instant response as companies with call centers

## What About Homeowners Who Prefer to Call?

The chatbot doesn't replace your phone — it complements it. Many homeowners prefer to chat first and call later. The chatbot warms them up, answers basic questions, and when they're ready to talk to a human, it provides your phone number or transfers the conversation to your team.

In fact, many roofers find that leads who chat first are higher quality because they've already shared their project details and confirmed they're in the service area.
