Here's a stat that should keep every roofing company owner up at night: the average contractor takes 47 hours to follow up on a new lead. Forty-seven hours. In a world where homeowners request estimates from 3-5 companies and go with whoever responds first, a 47-hour response time is a death sentence for your close rate.

Automated follow-ups fix this problem entirely. When a lead comes in — from your website, a Google ad, or a phone call — the system responds in under 1 minute with a personalized SMS and email. No human intervention needed.

## What Is Automated Follow-Up for Contractors?

Automated follow-up for contractors is a system that instantly responds to new leads and continues nurturing them through a series of timed messages until they book an appointment, close the deal, or opt out. It uses SMS, email, or both to maintain contact without requiring manual effort from your team.

Think of it as a tireless salesperson who follows up with every single lead at the perfect time, with the perfect message — without you having to remember or manage anything.

## Why Speed-to-Lead Matters for Roofers

The data on response time is clear and dramatic:

- **Responding in under 5 minutes** makes you 21x more likely to qualify the lead
- **Responding in under 1 minute** makes you 391% more likely to convert
- **After 30 minutes**, your chances of qualifying the lead drop by 21x
- **After 24 hours**, most leads have already hired someone else

For roofing companies, this is especially critical because homeowners requesting estimates typically have an urgent need — a leak, storm damage, or a home sale requiring a roof certification. They're going with the first responsive roofer, period.

## How Automated Follow-Up Works for Roofing Companies

A well-designed follow-up system handles the entire lead nurturing process:

### Instant Response (0-1 minute)

The moment a lead comes in, they receive:
- **SMS**: "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out to [Company]! We received your request for a roof estimate. One of our team members will be in touch shortly. Reply YES to confirm your appointment interest."
- **Email**: A professional confirmation with your company info, review highlights, and what to expect next

### Same-Day Follow-Up (2-4 hours)

If the lead hasn't responded or booked, a second touchpoint goes out:
- A brief SMS checking in and offering to answer questions
- An email with helpful content (like what to expect during a roof inspection)

### Next-Day Follow-Up (24 hours)

A friendly reminder that maintains urgency without being pushy:
- "Hi [Name], just following up on your roof estimate request. We have availability this week — would you like to schedule a time?"

### Extended Nurture (Days 3-14)

For leads that haven't converted, a drip sequence provides value and keeps you top-of-mind:
- Tips for identifying roof damage
- Information about insurance claim processes
- Customer success stories and reviews
- Limited-time offers or seasonal promotions

### Re-Engagement (30-90 days)

Even leads that go cold can come back. Periodic check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days catch homeowners whose timelines changed or who weren't ready initially.

## SMS vs. Email: Which Works Better for Roofers?

Both have their place, but SMS dramatically outperforms email for roofing leads:

- **SMS open rates**: 98% (vs. 20% for email)
- **SMS response rates**: 45% (vs. 6% for email)
- **Average SMS read time**: 3 minutes after delivery

The best approach uses both: SMS for immediate, time-sensitive communication and email for longer-form content and documentation.

## Common Objections (and Why They're Wrong)

### "Won't this annoy my customers?"

No. Homeowners who request a roofing estimate expect to hear back quickly. They're annoyed by slow responses, not fast ones. Every message includes an easy opt-out, and the system stops automatically when they book or respond.

### "I prefer the personal touch."

Automated doesn't mean impersonal. Messages are personalized with the lead's name, their specific project type, and your company's voice. Many leads can't tell the difference between an automated message and a manual one — and frankly, they don't care. They care about getting a response.

### "I already follow up manually."

How consistently? With every lead? Within 1 minute? Manual follow-up fails because humans get busy. You're on a roof. You're at dinner. You're asleep. Automation never forgets, never gets busy, and never takes a day off.
